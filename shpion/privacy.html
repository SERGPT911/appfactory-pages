Spy: Find the Outsider — Privacy Policy

Effective: 14 November 2026

What the app does with your data

Spy is an offline party game. Player names, your own locations, settings and the game archive (scores and history) are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. We do not have accounts or servers that receive your data. The app does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Yandex Advertising Network (YANDEX LLC): a banner on the main screens and occasional full-screen ads between games. Ads are never shown while roles are being dealt or during questioning and voting. To show and measure ads, the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK may process the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), device and app information, IP address and crash diagnostics. Processing is governed by Yandex's policy: https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/. You can deny or revoke tracking at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; the game works the same.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
