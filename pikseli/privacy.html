Pikseli — Privacy Policy

Effective: 14 November 2026

What the app does with your data

Pikseli is a mood diary. The colors, notes and tags you add are stored only on your device. A small summary of recent days is kept in the device keychain so the widget can show it. Nothing is sent to us; we have no accounts or servers that receive your data. You can export a CSV copy or delete days at any time; deleting the app removes the data.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Yandex Advertising Network (YANDEX LLC). To show and measure ads, the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK may process the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), device and app information, IP address and crash diagnostics. Your diary entries are never shared with the ad network. Processing is governed by Yandex's policy: https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/. You can deny or revoke tracking at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; the app works the same.

Notifications and Photos

The evening reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. Photos access is used only to save the year poster when you choose to; the app does not read your library.

Health

Pikseli is not a medical device and does not give medical or psychological advice.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
