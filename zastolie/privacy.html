Zastolie — Privacy Policy

Effective: 14 November 2026

What the app does with your data

Zastolie stores your guest count, menu, shopping list, cooking-plan check marks and holiday archive only on your device. Nothing is sent to us. We have no accounts and no servers that receive your data.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Yandex Advertising Network (YANDEX LLC). To show and measure ads, the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK may process the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), device and app information, IP address and crash diagnostics. Processing is governed by Yandex's policy: https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/. You can deny or revoke tracking at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; the app works the same.

Sharing

When you share the shopping list, the text is passed to the app you choose in the iOS share sheet. We do not receive it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Обработка регулируется политикой Яндекса: https://yandex.ru/legal/confidential/. Отслеживание можно запретить в «Настройки → Конфиденциальность и безопасность → Отслеживание» — приложение работает так же.
